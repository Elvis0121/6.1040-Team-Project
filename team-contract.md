# Team Contract

## 1. Expected Level of Achievement and Effort

- Each team member plans to contribute approximately **6 hours per week**.
- Our goal is to achieve an **Expert** level of achievement wherever possible, while being content with a **Competent** level in certain areas.

## 3. Team Meetings and Communication

- **Frequency:** Once a week.
- **Time:** Tentatively, 4:00 PM on Wednesdays, after the lecture.
- **Location:** Stud (subject to change).
- Meetings can be rescheduled or moved to Zoom when necessary.
- Team members should communicate effectively through text between meetings.

## 4. Maintaining Quality of Work

- During each team meeting, members will present the code and work they have completed since the previous meeting.
- Team members will discuss their progress and provide feedback on each other's work.
- Issues, improvements, and potential areas of concern will be discussed as a team.

## 5. Task Assignment and Missed Deadlines

- Tasks and components will be assigned ahead of time.
- Each team member will initially be assigned a component to design.
- Assigned concepts should be different to encourage a variety of ideas.
- Components can be assigned based on individual preferences or by using a random wheel.
- In some cases, two team members may independently design the same component. In these situations:
  1. Compare the proposed designs.
  2. Discuss the strengths and weaknesses of each design.
  3. Combine the best aspects of both designs where appropriate.
  4. Assign one person to implement the final agreed-upon design.
- If a deadline is likely to be missed, the team member should communicate this to the rest of the team as early as possible so tasks and priorities can be adjusted.

## 6. Decision-Making and Conflict Resolution

- Decisions will be made through team discussion.
- Team members will propose and discuss possible solutions before agreeing on an approach.
- The team will aim to reach a consensus when making important decisions.
- If the team cannot reach a consensus, we can involve our TA mentor to help resolve the disagreement.
